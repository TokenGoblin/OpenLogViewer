- **Every write permission lives in your layer** (from w3-connection). `EcuConnection` has no confirmation gate of its own, as in the C#. The view model's Send to ECU and Burn paths, their confirmations and any read-only handling (the MicroSquirt in a live car is used read-only by convention) must be ported exactly. Test that no UI path writes or burns without the confirmation the C# shows.
- **Warn about unburned and lost writes** (from the w3-connection fixes). `EcuConnection` now exposes `unburned_pages()`, `lost_pages()` and `reopens()`, and `burn_page` refuses after a reopen has lost the write ("Nothing was burned: the changes written to page N are lost…"). The C# has neither; it burned the unchanged tune and reported success. Show the refusal as an error, never as success. Warn before anything that reopens the port (reconnect, disconnect, switching ECU) while `unburned_pages()` is non-empty, and offer to re-send the lost changes.
- **Never call `EcuConnection::dispose` on the UI thread**: it waits for the request in flight, up to its timeouts (about 15 s worst case in `read_identity`).
